Legal Security

Our 4-Step Title & Due Diligence Guarantee

In Cameroon, land security depends on rigorous documentation. Here is how Fred's Global Real Estate guarantees unencumbered ownership for every single client.

01
Chief Attestation

Customary and traditional council endorsement from the recognized local authority in Buea, guaranteeing authentic ancestral pedigree without chieftaincy contestations.

02
Cadastral Demarcation

Licensed cadastral surveyors map GPS coordinates and plant permanent concrete boundary pillars, creating an approved Master Layout Site Plan registered with local municipal authorities.

03
Notarized Deed of Transfer

Execution of the official Deed of Assignment by a certified Notary Public in Buea, establishing an irrevocable, legally binding record of transfer between vendor and purchaser.

04
Registered Land Title

Submission and issuance of the official Land Certificate (Titre Foncier) through the regional Land Registry, giving you complete, unassailable freehold rights.
